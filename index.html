EVIDENCE / INFERENCE / INTERPRETATION

What you are seeing.

01

Actual photographs

Three published views of the restaurant and its threshold, photographed by Fernando Guerra. They retain the source composition, furniture and colours. The study examines the documented 2017 interior, not a verified present-day inventory.

02

Estimated depth

Depth Anything V2 Small estimates relative depth from each single image. A perspective-compensated mesh and three depth layers produce restrained parallax. This is an optical reconstruction, not surveyed geometry.

03

Architectural reading

Object regions are manually traced. Lines and diagrams describe observed relationships. Measured dimensions, hidden construction, lighting performance and original 3D renders are not available in this study.

Inspect the effect

Depth effect toggles between the moving reconstruction and the original still. Depth map displays the actual estimated map: white is nearer, black is farther. Layers separates depth bands as a diagram; gaps are deliberately left empty. It does not invent unseen furniture or surfaces.

Controls, accessibility & offline use

Mouse: move for parallax, drag for a small perspective shift. Touch: drag slowly to explore; a quick horizontal swipe changes scenes. Arrow keys change scenes; A toggles analysis, M toggles depth motion, P opens the plan, F toggles fullscreen, Esc closes a dialog or selection. Tab reaches every button and object. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. If WebGL is unavailable, the photographs and analysis remain usable in still mode. The application makes no network requests; source links open only when you choose them.
