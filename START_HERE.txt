EMILE — BETWEEN TIMBER & FOREST
An interactive photographic architectural study

WINDOWS
1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Emile_Cinematic folder.
3. Double-click START_EMILE.cmd.

The launcher opens index.html in your default browser. Microsoft Edge or
Google Chrome with hardware acceleration is recommended. You can also open
index.html directly. There is no installer, server, Python, Node.js, account,
or internet connection required to run the study.

HOW TO EXPLORE
Click "Explore the interior".
Move your mouse to see relative depth; drag gently for a limited change of
perspective. On a touchscreen, drag slowly to explore or swipe horizontally
and quickly to change scenes. Use the arrows at the bottom to navigate.

Click a numbered hotspot or its outlined object. The side panel explains the
furniture, material, lighting or spatial relationship. "Read the room" opens
12 analytical lenses. "Analysis" hides or shows the graphics.

Depth effect: toggle between inferred depth and the original still.
Depth map: inspect the actual precomputed AI map; white is nearer.
Layers: separate the three optical depth bands. Gaps are left empty.
Floor plan: explore zoning, seating, table counts and lighting zones.
Evidence & method: see sources, limitations and image-rights status.

KEYBOARD
Left / Right: change photographic scene
A: show / hide analytical overlays
M: turn depth effect on / off
P: open original floor plan
F: toggle fullscreen
Esc: close the current dialog or object selection
Tab / Enter: reach and activate controls and objects

IMPORTANT EVIDENCE AND RIGHTS LIMITS
The scene photographs are authentic; depth is inferred, not measured 3D.
Photograph redistribution permission has NOT been verified. This academic
analytical prototype does not grant any rights to reuse the photographs.
Read docs/RIGHTS.txt before public sharing, publication or redistribution.

If WebGL is unavailable, the experience automatically keeps the photographs
and analysis available in still mode. Source links only need internet if you
choose to open them. All presentation assets are already inside this folder.
